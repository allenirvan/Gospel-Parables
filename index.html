40-Day Gospel Parables Reading Plan

A Catholic-themed daily reading plan arranged in approximate chronological order according to Jesus’ public ministry.

“He who has ears, let him hear.” — Matthew 13:9

How to Use This Plan

Each day, read the passage slowly in the New Revised Standard Version Catholic Edition. Ask: What does this reveal about God? Then ask: What does this reveal about my own heart?

Close with a short prayer: Lord Jesus, give me ears to hear, a heart ready to repent, and the grace to live what You teach. Amen.
